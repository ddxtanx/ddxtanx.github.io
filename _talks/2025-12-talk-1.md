As a part of the University of Minnesota's Topology Group's ArXiV seminar, wherein we would present weekly on a recent paper,
I gave a talk on "The Descent Spectral Sequence for Topological Modular Forms" by Carrick et. al.
I discussed synthetic spectra, their applications towards providing more structure for spectral sequences, and 
presented the author's main ideas in how they tackled differentials in the aforementioned spectral sequence.
