Talk given as a part of a stable homotopy theory seminar organized by Prof. Vesna Stojanoska.
We discuss the MMSS approach to structured spectra by way of diagram spectra. 
We discuss the interpretation of sequential spectra in this setting,
as well as the defects that arise due to the failure of monoidality.
We then ameliorate this issue by moving to symmetric spectra,
and then discuss the prickly nature of the homotopy theory of those objects.
Again, we fix that issue by moving now to orthogonal spectra.
