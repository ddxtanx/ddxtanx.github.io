This talk was a part of the reading group on chromatic homotopy theory organize by Yigal Kamel
at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign. I discussed the geometry of MU theory,
its construction as a spectrum, 
and then delved into the high level proof of Quillen's theorem
focusing heavily on Hopf Algebroid structures and their intuitive properties.
