This talk was given as a part of a Kan Seminar organized by Prof. Charles Rezk.
I presented on "Vector Fields on Spheres" by Adams, going over the setup of the problem
as well as Adams' topological approach to obstructing the existence of nonvanishing vector fields
via stunted projective spaces.
