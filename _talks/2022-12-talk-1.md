My first ever talk! For a final presentation for MATH 533 (Fiber Bundles) at the University of Illinois Urbana-Champaign,
I gave a talk on the Serre Spectral Sequence and its use as a tool to compute cohomology groups of spaces related via some bundle structure.
I spent the latter portions of the talk computing the cohomology groups of the classifying spaces of the unitary groups
and constructing chern classes.
