Talk given to the University of Minnesota Topology Seminar. This was roughly on the work that I and the listed coauthors
did at the ICERM Machine Computation in Homotopy Theory workshop. 
Starting off with the basics of spectral sequence, we build up to the various kinds of structures in spectral sequence (multiplicativity, brackets, synthetic Leibniz, etc)
and then discuss how one might program a computer to automatically use the aforementioned structures to propagate differential information.
