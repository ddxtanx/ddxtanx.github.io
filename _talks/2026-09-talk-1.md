Talk given as the oral component of my "qualifying" exam. 
We trace through the classical notions of André-Quillen (co)homology for augmented algebras,
as well as the basics of equivariant homotopy theory and the generalization to the equivariant algebra
of (incomplete) Mackey/Green/Tambara functors.
We then discuss the work of Hill and Leeman in generalizing the classic notion of AQ (co)homology
to the setting of equivariant algebra across various notions of incompleteness.
We then discuss our directions for interpolating between the two approaches.
